## 1. Player Promise

**One-line promise**

> You are a gold miner. You mine ore and push it home to get rich, while rivals and bandits can rob your cart.

**One familiar comparison.**

It's like Sea of Thieves' "carry the loot back to the outpost", but it's a 2-minute mining run with a cart anyone can rob.

**Why this game**

We love the moment in multiplayer games when greed beats good sense: "one more rock" and then you get caught. Mining and hauling is simple enough to understand in seconds, and the glowing cart makes every player's risk visible to everyone else.

## 2. First Minutes & How to Play

| Time | Player experience |
|---|---|
| **0–5 seconds after control** | You spawn at Lanternhold base. Arrows point to Foreman Dusty, the Cart Dock holds five carts, and the open tunnel glows at the far end. |
| **5–10 seconds** | You talk to Dusty; he says three lines. You press E on a cart and it rolls in front of you. Goal: fill the cart, bank it. |
| **10–60 seconds** | You walk to Tunnel 1 and tap CLAIM on the board. In your private room you tap the 3×3 ore face; rocks burst and ore flies into your cart. |
| **1–3 minutes** | The face clears and a new one appears: "DEPTH 20 m". Cart full: you tap TAKE CART, then EXIT. The loaded cart slows you as you push past rivals to the vault and tap DEPOSIT. |
| **3–10 minutes** | You buy Pickaxe Lv2 at the Forge. Next trip: "Bandits on your tail!" Keep running, reach the safe zone, or spend stamina to scare them off? |
| **Natural stopping point** | You deposit. Coins, upgrades and depth are saved, and the quest board shows 2/3 done, with new jobs tomorrow. |

**Player-facing How to Play**

- Mine ore in your room into your cart.
- Push it to the vault to bank coins.
- Rivals and bandits rob carts outside.

## 3. Core Loop

| # | Step (verb) | What the player does (Player input → what they see or hear → what changes) | Why do it again? |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Mine** | Claim a private room, tap the 3×3 ore face → pickaxe swings, rocks burst → ore fills the cart; a cleared face is replaced, depth +10 m | Deeper faces hold richer ore |
| 2 | **Haul** | TAKE CART, EXIT, push it home → the heap glows, the loaded cart slows you, bandits may chase → choose Narrow Pass or side route | Bigger load = bigger payoff, bigger risk |
| 3 | **Deposit** | Reach the vault counter → panel lists ore × value → DEPOSIT: coins saved, the shared tunnel bar moves | Banked coins are safe forever |
| 4 | **Upgrade** | Forge → BUY → stronger pickaxe, bigger cart, more stamina | Deeper ore, bigger trips, more steals per trip |
| 5 — optional | **Steal** | Click or run into a rival's cart to grab; press E to ram → alert, ore spills → you gain, they chase | Revenge and named rivals |

| | |
|---|---|
| **One complete loop takes** | About 90 seconds. Completion = "Weighed and paid: +coins" at the vault; the first payoff is the first rock, after about 5 seconds. |
| **Decision, challenge, or expression** | When to stop mining and go home; how to spend stamina (mine, grab, ram or scare bandits); which route; which upgrade; whether to rob, run or take revenge. |
| **Shortest satisfying visit / typical session** | 3 / 10–15 minutes |
| **Why repetition 10 remains appealing** | Other players change every trip: who is nearby, who carries a rich cart, who robbed you. Bandit raids hit at random times and from different sides, and deeper faces bring new ore. |

**Pillars**

1. Greed vs safety — every trip is a bet.
2. Every cart is a target — players and bandits create the stories.
3. One shared haul — every ton fills the tunnel bar.

## 4. Why Players Come Back

### 4.1 The next-day (D1) sentence

> "A player who enjoyed their first session returns the next day (D1) because three new daily quests and their streak reward are waiting (Hook 1), which they know from the quest board shown at their last deposit."

### 4.2 The progression chain

> **Mine and haul → coins → pickaxe, cart and boots upgrades → deeper, richer faces and bigger hauls → bigger targets, rivals and revenge**

| Moment | What persists or has been built? | What becomes possible next? | How can another player tell? |
|---|---|---|---|
| **End of first session** | Coins, Pickaxe Lv2, saved depth, quest progress | Silver ore and the risky haul through the Narrow Pass | Silver in the cart heap, cart trim band |
| **End of first week** | Lv3–4 gear, ~100 m depth, a named rival, a tunnel badge | Gold ore and gems, bounty hunting, the next random tunnel | Steel cart model, gold-glowing heap, badge on name tag |
| **Week 3+ — what takes more than two weeks?** | Lv5 gear (~27,800 coins), tunnel badges (3 in V1, 5 in V2), Pioneer titles | Crystal Heart season finale (V2), weekly league titles | Brass cart with lanterns, badges, crown on the leaderboard |

At the end of your first week you take a cart and it is your steel Lv4 cart. You mine 110 m deep, break a gem out of a gold face, and push 80 glowing tons out of the tunnel. Marta, who robbed you yesterday, waits at the Narrow Pass; you take the side route, then ram her on the next trip. Tunnel 3 just opened, and your Tunnel 1 badge shows on your name tag.

Coins are earned by depositing ore and finishing quests and spent on upgrades (later cosmetics); they cannot be traded or withdrawn. Main abuse risk: alt accounts feeding ore — the server checks every steal, and robbing the same victim repeatedly pays less.

### 4.3 Two return hooks

| Selected hook | Exact trigger or timing | What the player anticipates | Reminder channel + no-reminder fallback |
|---|---|---|---|
| **1.** Daily quests + streak (V1) | 3 new quests at 00:00 UTC; streak rewards on day 3, 7 and 14; 1 rest token per week | New jobs, coins and the next streak reward | Quest board at every deposit; Community post — fallback: the board shows "New jobs in 6h" |
| **2.** Tunnel rotation (V1) | When all players deposit 100,000 t (or after 7 days), the tunnel seals; after 1 hour of maintenance a random unopened tunnel opens (V1: Tunnels 1–3; V2 adds Tunnel 4 and Crystal Heart as the finale); depth resets to 0 m | Seeing which tunnel is next, racing deep again, earning its badge and the Pioneer title | "ALL MINERS' HAUL" bar on the HUD, maintenance countdown; Event listing — fallback: the bar shows "92%" at the last visit |

## 5. Social by Design

| | |
|---|---|
| **The repeatable social loop** | Player A hauls a rich, glowing cart → Player B grabs, crashes into or rams it → A runs, chases or steals back → "Revenge: B" stays on A's HUD (+50% when stealing back), giving them a reason to meet again. |
| **The disappearance test** | Stealing, rivals, revenge and the shared tunnel goal break. Mining, bandits, banking and upgrades still work, but the human risk that makes every trip a story is gone. |
| **From strangers to a group** | Individual game, no crews. Every cart shows its owner's name and load and glows by value, so a newcomer sees within seconds who is rich, who is a target and who robbed whom. |
| **Recognition & continuity** | Names appear above every cart and in alerts ("Marta is grabbing your ore!"). Your last robber stays as your Revenge target and changes who you chase next session; leaderboards come in V1. |
| **Quiet hours & player counts** | With nobody else online, a solo player enjoys the greed-vs-time choice because bandit raids still chase every loaded haul and deeper faces still pay more. If no earlier-player traces exist yet, the shared tunnel bar still shows everyone's progress. Social play becomes viable at 2 players; the ideal group is 4–8; the target player count is 8 players. Tested count: local Preview with 2 accounts on desktop; 4-player World playtest next. A solo arrival meets others by design: there is one tunnel, one vault and two routes home, so every hauler crosses paths. |
| **Drop-in / drop-out** | A late arrival gets a cart and claims a free room within a minute, and can steal right away. If someone leaves, their cart ore is lost and play continues; griefing is limited by the safe zone, the rookie rule, immunity, stamina costs and server checks. |
| **Visible play (the bystander test)** | Anyone watching sees miners pushing glowing carts home while rivals and bandits close in. |
| **Shareable play (the memorable moment)** | Ramming a rival's 80-ton gold cart in the tunnel yard and watching everyone dive for the spilled pile. |
| **Bring-a-friend** | The moment a friend robs you, you want to get even. Friends become named rivals, racing each other's depth and coins. |

## 6. Mobile-First

**Every core-loop verb on touch.**

| Core-loop verb | How it works with touch controls |
|---|---|
| Mine | Action button on a dock cart (or the Equip Cart button), tap CLAIM on the tunnel board, then tap the ore face (one swing per 0.6 s) |
| Haul | TAKE CART button, then EXIT; move with the joystick, the cart rides in front |
| Deposit | Walk to the vault counter, tap DEPOSIT |
| Upgrade | Forge or "Upgrades ▸", big BUY buttons |
| Steal | Tap a rival's cart or run into it to grab; action button to ram |

**UI plan.** On phones the UI is drawn about 25% smaller and only stamina, cart load, tunnel bar and coins stay on screen, with big buttons and nothing to read before playing. Other buttons sit behind a More ▾ button, and station panels open folded as small tabs on the right.

**Performance.** Target 60 fps on desktop and 30 fps on mobile at 8 players. Planned test device/client: `TBD:` a mid-range Android phone on the Decentraland mobile client, confirmed during the V0 playtest.

**Desktop-only dependencies.** None. Cart weight and TIRED change movement speed through `AvatarLocomotionSettings`, which works on mobile.

## 7. World, Look & Story

**Story / world**

The Old Shaft under the mining town of Lanternhold has reopened after a hundred years. The Mining Company pays for every ton delivered to its vault — and never asks whose cart it came from.

**Visual direction.** Chunky low-poly canyon with a pink-orange sunset sky and purple cliffs; the navy-and-brass vault is the landmark, and dirt paths (no rails) lead to it. Ore is colour-coded (copper orange, silver grey, gold amber, gem purple) and glows in the cart, and bandits wear red tags, so targets and threats read clearly on small screens. Signature screenshot: a line of glowing, overloaded carts in the Narrow Pass with bandits closing in.

## 8. Audience & Comparables

**Primary player + arrival context**

> For players who already enjoy quick competitive multiplayer games, arriving alone or with friends, looking for short sessions with real stakes and steady progression.

**How the first group arrives**

Weekly "Rush Hour" Events and a Decentraland Community for miners bring the first group in at the same time. Players arriving at other times still meet, because one tunnel, one vault and two routes keep everyone crossing paths.

**Deliberately not for**

Players looking for a calm, purely cooperative farming or building game.

### Comparables

| | Comparable A — Decentraland if known; otherwise outside: Sea of Thieves | Comparable B — outside Decentraland: Agar.io |
|---|---|---|
| What we observed works | Carrying loot home while others can steal it creates tension and stories | Instantly understandable "grow and risk losing it" loop; short rounds |
| What does not fit our audience or context—and why | Long sessions, complex controls and ship crews don't fit short mobile Decentraland visits | Losing everything at once frustrates and leaves no lasting progression |
| What we will do differently | 90-second trips, tap-only controls, a robbery takes part of a cart, not everything | Banked coins are safe forever; upgrades, depth and tunnels give long-term goals |

## 9. 4 Week Plan (v1 scope)

V0, already deployed: Cart Dock, 4 Claim Rooms, rolling ore face with saved depth, pickaxe, cart weight, stamina, Deposit and Forge, grab, crash grab, ram, bandit raids and ambush, tutorial, mobile layout and saves on the Multiplayer Server.

| Week | What is playable / done |
|---|---|
| 1 — Prototype definition | 4-player World playtest on desktop and mobile; fixes from the playtest |
| 2 — Core interaction + first-group test | Obstacles to shake off bandits, cart tier models, rooms 5–8, 8-player test |
| 3 — Core systems refinement | Tunnels 2 (Flooded Works: puddles slow carts) and 3 (Lava Vent: long hauls lose ore) reusing the same rooms and rules, tunnel rotation between Tunnels 1–3, daily quests + streak, bounty crown and weekly leaderboards |
| 4 — Playable release, including testing and deployment | Balance pass, audio, mobile 30 fps check, deploy, launch Event |

**What keeps the experience changing after launch**

- Without building a new level, variation comes from random bandit raids and who is online. With rotation, the open tunnel changes every 3–7 days within our support capacity.
- If an update is skipped, daily quests and player robberies still create variation.
- Depth resets with each new tunnel while upgrades persist; a new player can contribute within 2 minutes by banking copper into the shared tunnel bar, protected by the rookie rule.
- One player behaviour that would change what we build next: if most players never go past 30 m, we make deeper faces pay more and the haul home less punishing.

**Not building in v1**

Tunnels 4–5 (planned for V2), Lantern upgrade and lamp oil, wall colour by depth, sabotage items (oil, crates, dynamite), Mother Lode event, season track, cosmetics shop. If v1 hits its numbers, Tunnels 4 (Ice Cavern) and 5 (Crystal Heart) come first in v2. Standing non-goal: never real-money or crypto payouts for ore, because it would turn robbery into a farming exploit.

**Top risk + fallback.**

Performance with 8 players, moving carts and up to 6 bandits on mobile. Fallback: carts are drawn locally from small synced data, bandit count scales down, and we keep 4–6 players per scene until the 30 fps test passes.
